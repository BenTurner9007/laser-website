    About our contour coasters

    Engraved coasters of your favourite hills

    TurnerMadeUK makes wooden coasters laser-engraved with a contour map of any hills in Great Britain or Ireland. Each coaster is about 100 mm across, in 2 mm ceiba plywood, and comes round, square or rounded square.

    You choose the area on the map above, and can add hill names, rivers, lakes and paths, a special place and your own words. Coasters can be ordered singly or as a set of up to four of the same design, and each extra coaster in a set costs a little less.

    They make a small gift for walkers: a favourite fell, a first Munro, or the hill behind the house. Questions and answers · How to design your map
